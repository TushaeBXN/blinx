# Blinx Mission

**"We're building the AI operations team every small nonprofit deserves — so the people doing the hardest good work can stop drowning in administration and lead their mission."**

## What We're Building

Blinx is a full-stack AI operating system for small and mid-sized nonprofits. It pairs a six-agent AI workforce — strategist, marketer, grant architect, inbox manager, freelance scout, and bookkeeper — with purpose-built modules for grants, donor CRM, compliance, contractors, social, time tracking, and more, all sharing one source of truth and a transparent approval queue for every outbound action.

## Where We're Headed

Imagine a Sunday evening at a small food bank. The director opens her laptop and instead of seventy-three unread emails she sees one approval queue. The CEO agent ranked them overnight, the grant architect flagged a Friday deadline, the bookkeeper surfaced a 1099 needing filing. She approves eighteen items in twenty minutes. The rest of her week belongs to the farmers she's building supply chains with.

That's the world we're building. A four-person team with the leverage of a forty-person one — where compliance, donor pipelines, grant pipelines, contractor payments, and social all run on one system that learns the organization. Where "executive" means executive again, not Director of Email Triage. Where a $300K clinic competes for the same foundation grants as a $30M hospital, because finding, applying, and tracking them is no longer bottlenecked by headcount.

Every nonprofit with fewer than fifty staff will soon have an onboard AI workforce — and Blinx will be the platform that made it feel like hiring their first great ops team. Every grant won, every donor retained, every deadline met — they compound.

## Core Principle

Every AI action that affects the outside world — an email to a donor, a social post, a grant submission, a contractor payment — sits in an approval queue with the full payload preview before it fires. Blinx augments human judgment; it never replaces it. **Outbound without review: 0.**
